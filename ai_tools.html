Avoid Chemical Sprays
Chemical pesticide and fertilizer sprays should be suspended. Immediate rains will wash away components into groundwater pools, creating losses.
Activate Drainage Channels
Ensure that all crop fields (particularly potato tubers and pulses) have clear, unclogged drainage trenches to prevent crop waterlogging and root rot diseases.
Delay Ripened Harvests
If grain crops (wheat/rice) are ripe, postpone manual mowing till dry atmospheric conditions. Freshly cut grain will quickly mold if damp.
